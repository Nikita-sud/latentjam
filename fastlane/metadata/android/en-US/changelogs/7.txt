SMART no longer stops. When the recommender cannot answer — while the library is still being analysed, or after a hiccup it used to hide — playback continues with tracks you have not heard, labelled in the queue as what they are, and real recommendations resume the moment it can. Several silences behind that are fixed outright, so a fully analysed library keeps its queue filled. Deleting a track now works on every supported Android version instead of failing silently below Android 11, and says so when it cannot. Language in Settings opens where the language can actually be changed on your system.
