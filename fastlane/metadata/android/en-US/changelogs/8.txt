The app rebuilt around one shell: a single Settings entry, page titles that slide with the pager, a search header that stays where you left it, and a background cloud drawn from the current artwork across browsing, search, collections and settings. Every browsing page shares one sort control and a floating Shuffle all that stays put while pages swipe. A floating mini-player carries artwork, transport and a progress line, and grows with larger text instead of clipping it. Playlists can wear a cover you choose. The three home-screen widgets are rebuilt to hold their shape as text and size change, and cost nothing while they sit still. The map page scrolls at any window size, with two fingers for pan and zoom, and keeps its lens, region and viewport across tabs and rotation. SMART no longer applies a recommendation the queue has already moved past; on iOS a pause you asked for survives a track change, repeat-one keeps the lock screen honest, and cover art is decoded off the main thread.
