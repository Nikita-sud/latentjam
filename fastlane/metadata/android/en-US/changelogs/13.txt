SMART picks better: smoother transitions, fewer jumps between genres, another version of a song you just heard counts as the same song, and playlists you mark “Keep together in SMART” stay together without abrupt changes. A new Artist variety slider (Settings › SMART engine) sets how often one artist may play in a row. The audio model is smaller and faster: the library is analysed once more after the update, up to twice as fast as before. The download is up to a third smaller.
