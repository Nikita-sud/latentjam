mnv4_audio.onnx is a pruned copy of a 960-dimensional MobileNetV4-Conv-M
student initialized from the previous EfficientAT MN10 distillation, then
retrieval-distilled with MN10 preservation, EfficientAT DyMN20 relational
supervision, and public playlist-transition supervision. This experimental
weight file has not yet passed the training-data rights review required for a
public release.
The pruned copy keeps half of the expanded channels of each of its 21
inverted-residual blocks and half of the head convolution's 1,280 hidden
units: those with the largest mean activation on calibration audio, weighted
by the size of the weights that read them. Its log-mel DFT is computed as a
four-step FFT (two 32-point stages of matrix products) instead of a
convolution over stored kernels; the math is the same. Its 45 pointwise
convolutions and its final projection hold symmetric 4-bit weights and run on
LatentJam's own operator (core/ort-ops): the 44 convolutions over time and
frequency with a float scale per output channel over a clipping range learned
in training, the head convolution and the projection, which run once per
window, in blocks of 32 inputs with a bfloat16 scale each. The pruned network
was trained by quantization-aware distillation, learning to reproduce the
unpruned float encoder's embeddings of 10 s windows from 8,222 Free Music
Archive tracks released under CC BY, CC0 or public domain (no share-alike or
no-derivatives licence), then fine-tuned for five epochs against the same
float encoder with the graph's INT8 convolutions and uint8 activations
simulated, on one 10 s crop from each of 15,000 tracks: 1,734 Free Music
Archive tracks under the same licences and 13,266 Million Playlist Dataset
preview clips outside the evaluation libraries. The graph is built from it by
tools/research/model_diet/qat_audio.py, distill_audio_cached.py and
make_q4_encoder.py. Its other convolution weights are INT8 (per-channel); its
activation ranges are the ones learned in that fine-tune where the training
simulates them, and otherwise calibrated on the first 10 s of 200 Free Music
Archive tracks under CC BY, CC BY-SA, CC0 or public domain; no audio is stored
in the model. Its track embeddings sit at a mean cosine of 0.992 to the
unpruned float encoder's, against 0.997 for the previous, unpruned 4-bit
graph.
predictor_state.onnx and predictor_scorer_n100.onnx store their matrix
weights as 4-bit blocks (ONNX Runtime's MatMulNBits: 32 weights per block,
each with an FP16 scale and a 4-bit zero point; the state net's GRU stays
FP16). They were fine-tuned from the FP32 weights by quantization-aware
distillation on inputs recorded from LatentJam's queue builder running over
libraries assembled from Million Playlist Dataset playlists and their preview
audio: with every music vector in those inputs replaced by the pruned
encoder's vector of the same track, they learned to reproduce the FP32 nets'
outputs on the original recordings (tools/research/model_diet). Those
recordings, like the music encoder's training data, have not passed the
rights review required for a public release.
universal_semantic_head.onnx is a compact 27-label routing head over those
960-dimensional embeddings. It preserves the calibrated FMA genre branch and
the EfficientAT MN10 hidden projection while pruning the AudioSet teacher from
527 outputs to the 59 source concepts needed for genre, mood, energy, speech,
sound effects, novelty-proxy, and instrumental scores. Its FMA-derived branch
has not passed the source-audio rights review required for public
redistribution. The novelty output is only a weak acoustic proxy and the app
requires metadata corroboration before naming a meme/viral mix. Its two
largest matrices are 4-bit blocks (MatMulNBits), fine-tuned to reproduce the
FP16 head's outputs on audio vectors of the same playlist libraries and of
Free Music Archive tracks.

artist_knowledge.bin holds one product-quantized 384-dimensional vector per
artist of music_entities_250k.bin, so the two files ship together. Each vector
is the MiniLM text encoder's embedding (sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2,
Apache-2.0, run offline) of a one-line description of
the artist that DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash wrote from the artist's name alone; the
description text is not included. Each record also keeps a 16-bit fingerprint
of the artist's normalized name, up to two language indices, the middle of the
decades the model says the artist was most active in, and the model's own
confidence; the file keeps records only for the artists whose confidence the
app accepts. artist_adapter.bin is a two-layer network trained by LatentJam
that guesses such a vector from a track's metadata vector when the pack does
not cover the artist. It was trained only on metadata strings synthesized from
the same model's structured artist attributes (languages, decades, genres),
never on a listener's library. Its weights are 4-bit codes (groups of 32
rotated by a Walsh-Hadamard matrix, a Lloyd-Max codebook and an FP16 length
per group), quantized after training. DeepSeek's terms of use permit using its
outputs, including for training.

music_entities_250k.bin is a hash index over artist names and aliases: the
250,000 MusicBrainz artists with the most artist credits, then musicians,
singers and groups from Wikidata that have a Wikipedia article and fall
outside that list, ranked by article count. MusicBrainz core data and Wikidata
are both CC0. The index stores only 40-bit name hashes and integer ids, never
the names themselves.

text_encoder.onnx is a three-layer, 256-wide BERT that LatentJam trained to
reproduce the mean-pooled sentence vectors of
sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2 (Apache-2.0,
https://huggingface.co/sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2); it contains
none of MiniLM's weights. text_vocab.txt is its own WordPiece vocabulary. The
training strings were artist names and aliases from the entity corpus above
(CC0), metadata strings synthesized from the DeepSeek artist attributes,
search-style phrases built from templates, MiniLM's vocabulary words, and
search phrases DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash translated into twenty languages. No
listener's library went into it. Its word embeddings are 4-bit blocks (ONNX
Runtime's GatherBlockQuantized), quantized after training, and its other
weights dynamic INT8.
